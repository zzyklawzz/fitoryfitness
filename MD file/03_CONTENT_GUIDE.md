# Purpose

This document defines the global writing standards for the Fitory website.

It ensures every page communicates with a consistent tone, style, and brand voice.

This guide applies to all website pages unless a page-specific content guide states otherwise.

---

# 1. Brand Voice

Fitory is a premium fitness brand.

The brand voice should always be:

- Premium
- Professional
- Modern
- Motivational
- Trustworthy
- Friendly
- Confident

Avoid sounding:

- Pushy
- Overly sales-driven
- Exaggerated
- Unprofessional

---

# 2. Writing Philosophy

Every piece of content should answer one question:

**"How does this benefit the member?"**

Focus on outcomes instead of features.

Example

❌ Premium Equipment

✅ Train with premium equipment designed to help you perform at your best.

---

# 3. Writing Style

Always use:

- Active voice
- Clear English
- Short paragraphs
- Easy-to-read sentences

Keep sentences between 10–20 words whenever possible.

Avoid long paragraphs.

---

# 4. Tone of Voice

The website should inspire confidence.

Readers should feel:

- Motivated
- Welcome
- Supported
- Excited to start

Never use pressure-selling language.

---

# 5. Headlines

Headlines should be:

- Short
- Powerful
- Benefit-driven
- Easy to scan

Examples

Train Smarter

Recover Better

Build Your Strength

Everything You Need

Your Fitness Journey Starts Here

---

# 6. Section Labels

Always use:

- UPPERCASE
- Short phrases
- One to four words

Examples

PREMIUM TRAINING

FITPRO ZONE

GROUP CLASSES

WHY FITORY

---

# 7. Body Content

Every paragraph should explain:

1. What it is.
2. Why it matters.
3. How it benefits the member.

Keep paragraphs concise.

Avoid unnecessary technical terms.

---

# 8. Feature Descriptions

Describe experiences instead of specifications.

Instead of listing equipment, explain how members benefit from using it.

Focus on:

- Better training
- Faster recovery
- Convenience
- Community
- Long-term fitness

---

# 9. Call-To-Action

Preferred CTA phrases:

- Get The App
- Explore Membership
- Join Today
- Start Your Journey
- Book A Visit
- Learn More

Avoid:

- Click Here
- Submit
- Buy Now
- Cheap Membership
- Best Gym

---

# 10. Button Text

Buttons should:

- Use Title Case
- Begin with an action verb
- Be short and clear

Examples

Get The App

Explore Membership

Book Now

View Classes

Contact Us

---

# 11. Bullet Lists

Keep bullet points short.

Preferred

✓ Premium Equipment

✓ Certified Coaches

✓ Recovery Facilities

✓ Group Classes

Avoid writing long sentences inside bullet lists.

---

---

Use language that reflects the Fitory brand.

Examples

---

# 14. Words to Avoid

Avoid overused marketing words.

Examples

Cheap

Best Ever

Guaranteed

Perfect

Ultimate

Revolutionary

Magic

No.1

---

# 15. SEO Writing

Write naturally.

Do not force keywords into sentences.

Content should always prioritize readability over keyword density.

---

# 16. Accessibility

Use clear language.

Avoid jargon.

Keep paragraphs short.

Use descriptive headings.

Buttons should clearly describe their action.

---

# 17. AI Content Rules

When generating website content:

- Follow the Fitory brand voice.
- Maintain a premium tone.
- Focus on benefits before features.
- Keep content concise.
- Avoid repeating the same wording.
- Avoid exaggerated marketing claims.
- Ensure content is production-ready.

---

# 18. Final Checklist

Before publishing any page, confirm:

✓ Tone matches the Fitory brand.

✓ Content is easy to scan.

✓ Benefits are clearly explained.

✓ Headlines are concise.

✓ CTA is action-oriented.

✓ Grammar and capitalization are consistent.

✓ Content aligns with the Design System.

✓ Components follow the Component Library.
