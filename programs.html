EVERY WORKOUT COUNTS.

Community leaderboards give members another way to stay motivated, recognise consistent effort and celebrate progress together.

Through the Burn Calories Community Milestone Campaign, members can track their Calorie Miles and see how their individual efforts contribute to the wider Fitory community. Every workout adds to the journey, turning personal progress into a shared achievement.

Monthly rankings highlight members who consistently put in the effort, creating friendly competition while encouraging everyone to stay active and keep moving.
